Bulk Payroll Actions

Select multiple payroll records from the table above to apply actions at once. Hover over each button for details.

  • Approve Selected: Marks all chosen records as approved and moves them to the 'Approved' step.
  • Reject Selected: Rejects records and flags them for review by HR or Finance.
  • Export Selected: Downloads payroll data in preferred format for offline review.
  • Upload Batch: Allows importing bulk payroll data with validation checks.
